\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \emph{tactile analogies}, the task of synthesizing tactile videos at untouched
locations from reference touches, and proposed an example-driven pipeline that retrieves the most
relevant reference with self-supervised features of normal renderings, aligns it to the query pose
with a pre-trained feature matcher, and refines the warped video with a lightweight network
conditioned on the query geometry and the press timestamp. On a benchmark built from ObjectFolder it
outperforms all tested baselines while staying fast enough for interactive use, and we outline how
its predictions could support contact-free 3D reconstruction and tactile simulation.
