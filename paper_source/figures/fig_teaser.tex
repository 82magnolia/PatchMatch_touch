  \caption{\textbf{Tactile analogies.} \emph{Left:} the object of interest, with the location of a
    reference touch we have already made boxed in blue and a new, previously untouched query
    location boxed in orange. \emph{Middle:} the surface normal render at each of the two sensor
    poses, covering a field of view four times the sensor footprint, where the red box marks the
    patch the sensor itself covers. \emph{Right:} the corresponding tactile normal videos, four
    frames spanning a press that pushes into the surface and then withdraws, with time running
    left to right. We represent a touch as a \emph{tactile normal video}, which records how the
    sensor's deformable gel bends over the course of the press, so what we predict is a short
    video rather than a single frame. Given the reference touch and the geometry at both
    locations, our method predicts the tactile signal that the sensor would measure at the query
    location, shown on the bottom right. The relationship between the two rows is the analogy our
    method must transfer, namely the relationship between the reference geometry and the reference touch, 
    which should be seamlessely mapped to reflect that between the query geometry and the touch we synthesize.}
  \label{fig:teaser}
