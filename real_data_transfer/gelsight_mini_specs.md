## Overview

The GelSight Mini is a commercially available tactile sensor with spatial resolution beyond human touch. It uses GelSight's patented elastomeric gel that conforms to surface topography, enabling instant 2D/3D visualization regardless of material reflectivity, transparency, or lighting conditions.

## Thermal Behavior

Tested under worst-case conditions at full frame rate (25 FPS) at typical indoor temperature. The measured temperature delta on the device case stabilizes below ~22.7 °C above ambient after sustained operation.

---

## Software & Interface

- **Platform support:** Linux (Ubuntu 18/20/22 LTS), Windows, macOS
- **ROS support:** ROS 1 Noetic Ninjemys, ROS 2 Foxy Fitzroy
- **PyTouch:** Compatible with the open-source tactile library for robotics
- **Connection:** USB Micro-B to USB-C (2 m cable included)
- **Web app features:**
  - Live 2D image view
  - Capture button and photo/video sharing
  - US coin identification (quarter, dime, nickel, penny)
  - Metric thread pitch detection (M3×0.5, M4×0.7, M5×0.8, M6×1.0)
  - Gel marker overlay for shear force estimation
